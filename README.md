Organisation Overview: 

K’s Ice Cream is founded by a third generation ‘Ice-cream enthusiast, Kwandah Simamane, back in late May of 2022. The store has grown significantly since then due to its limitless and creative ice cream flavours, desserts, and vegan options. K’s Ice Cream goal is to create high quality, delicious, both warm and cool treats that would leave an ever-lasting impression on our valued customer’s taste buds from the ages of 10-40 years of age, with outliers of course. We, K’s Ice cream, also aim to be our community’s backbone creating joyful memories and offering emotional and financial support to the community in which we cherish dearly. 

 

Website Goals and Objectives: 

K’s Ice cream website goals are to increase website traffic, cultivate a loyal online community, and display our goodies the best way we can. 

K’s Ice cream website objectives are to build an email subscriber list of 1500 customers and generate 15% of sales through online orders. 

 

Current Website Analysis: 

Not applicable 

 

Proposed Website Features and Functionality: 

Websites feature that will be included by K’s Ice Cream is the integration of social media links, such as TikTok and Instagram to increase online engagement. 

A more interactive menu will be installed with higher image quality and a user-friendly menu with creative descriptions. 

A loyalty program will be included so returning customers can get various discounts and points for a point system that will gift them distinct rewards. 

 

 

 

Design and User Experience: 

The design elements of K’s ice cream include dark, pastel colour scheme with hints of light shading and simple navigation menus. 

The user experience is easy checkout process, a mobile friendly layout, and fast loading pages.
